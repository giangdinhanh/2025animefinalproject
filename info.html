Core fieldsScore · Members · Rank

Key definitions

Score is the current audience rating. Members represents users tracking an anime. Scored By is the number of users contributing a rating. Rank and Popularity use lower numbers for stronger positions.

Limitations

MyAnimeList users do not represent the entire global anime audience.
Members and Scored By may be strongly correlated.
Predictions are estimates based on historical patterns.
The output should support, not replace, business judgment.
